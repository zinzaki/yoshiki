depth steps

06 effects

Optional richness, by place.

These are not part of the kit, because most surfaces do not want them. They ship as separate files and obey four rules: one effect per surface, always warm, only where a pointer and a GPU are, and a fallback is mandatory. Anything for reading or working — the terminal, code, dense data, body text — stays flat.

frosted card

Warm ink, never white glass.

A gold hairline, one specular pass, and a solid surface under prefers-reduced-transparency.

The glass is made of the raised surface and the specular is whichever way the light falls, so it holds on paper as well as on lacquer — switch the theme and watch.
