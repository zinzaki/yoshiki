01 the one law

Colour earns its power by being rare.

A signal used everywhere is dead. So a screen is almost all quiet tone, and the rare strike lands like a seal — chosen by meaning, never by taste. The budget below is not a style preference; it is the mechanism.

tone
lacquer carries the screen — ~70%
bone
text and the surfaces it sits on — ~20%
gold
seams, edges, glyphs — ~7%, never a fill
trigger
scarlet 彼岸花 · moss 森 — ~3%, by meaning only
02 the gold law

Gold is the seam, never the slab.

No one would wear a solid-gold watch — but a gilded edge is precious. So gold lives only as line, edge, frame or glyph. The largest gold surface the language allows is a 2px stroke or a single mark. A primary action is a gold-framed tone surface with gold text, and the difference is not subtle once you see the two together.

right — the edge is the affordance

A tone surface, a 1.5px gold edge, gold text. It reads as primary because nothing else on the surface wears an edge — hierarchy by rarity, not by area.

✗ wrong — gold as a fill

The same gold, spent as a slab. It shouts, it flattens the warmth of the screen, and by the third button of its kind it has stopped meaning anything. This is the one shape the kit will not give you.

Scarlet is the exception that proves it: scarlet may fill, because the fill is itself the rare event. One per screen, on the thing you cannot undo.

03 two themes, one language

Only the stage changes.

kogane 黄金 is warm lacquer; washi 和紙 is paper and sumi ink. The triggers and every rule are identical — what moves is which surfaces carry the light and which family carries the text. Both blocks below are the same markup, wearing the two themes at once.

04 the palette

Every shade has a job — and a proof.

Raw tokens are the paint; roles are the contract. Consume text.body or action.edge, never a raw hex — a role guarantees the right contrast in both themes, on every surface it can land on. The build fails if one cell drops below its floor.

06 one source

Thirteen targets, generated.

Nothing downstream is maintained by hand. One palette file is the source; the build bakes every theme, every token export, this site's colours, and the contrast proof — then CI re-runs it and fails on any drift. A palette that can rot is not a design system.

07 point an agent at it

A repository that steers the model.

Give an agent the link and nothing else. It reads AGENTS.md, learns the read order and the laws, then loads only the module its task needs — palette, CLI, components, comments, chat, image prompts. There is also a machine index at llms.txt and W3C design tokens for the tooling that wants structure instead of prose.
