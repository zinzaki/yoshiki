# skeleton — the shape of what is coming, before it arrives.
# In text, a skeleton is a rhythm of blocks at the real widths, pulsing
# between about 60% and 100% opacity over ~1.6s. Never a sweeping shimmer:
# a shimmer is decoration pretending to be progress.
