# Glyph sets — curated rows by meaning. One glyph, one job.
# Pick from a row; never reuse a glyph for two meanings on one screen.

## by meaning
 status     ✓ ✗ ● ○ ◉ ◌ ⊘ ⏻ ⟳ ↺ ◐ ▫ ⋯
 verdict    ✓ ⚠ ✗ ⊝ ⋯                    passed · flaky · failed · skipped · pending
 flow       → ← ↑ ↓ ↦ ↻ ⇄ ⇅ » « ↳ ⤷
 rank       ◆ ◇ ◈ ▲ ▼ ★ ☆ ⬡ ⬢ ▵ ▪
 field      · — │ ┃ ┊ ┆ ╌ ┄
 sigil      ⧉ ⛬ ⊙ ◷ ❖ ⌁ ⌂ ✦ ⚑
 bracket    [ ] ( ) ⟨ ⟩ « » ⟦ ⟧ ◸ ◿
 corner     ╭ ╮ ╰ ╯ ┌ ┐ └ ┘ ╔ ╗ ╚ ╝
 join       ├ ┤ ┬ ┴ ┼ ╞ ╡
 fill       ▰ ▱ ░ ▒ ▓ ▁ ▂ ▃ ▄ ▅ ▆ ▇
 spinner    ⠋ ⠙ ⠹ ⠸ ⠼ ⠴ ⠦ ⠧ ⠇ ⠏         braille, for live ticks
 braille    ⠀ ⡀ ⡄ ⡆ ⡇ ⣇ ⣧ ⣷ ⣿           a density ramp, eight steps

## the pairs that must not be mixed
 ✓ / ✗      verdict          ●  / ○     state
 ▲ / ▼      direction        ⚠         judgement
 ↑ ▲ is not "good" and ● is not "passed". A glyph that carries two meanings
   on one screen carries neither.

## tiers — pick the lowest that does the job
 tier 0    ✓ ✗ ● ◆ → ▲ ▼ · — │ ⧉        any font, anywhere, no exceptions
 tier 1    ◇ ○ ◉ ⟳ ↻ ⊘ ★ ▰ ▱ ╭ ╰ ┤      common Unicode, safe in practice
 tier 2    ⣿ ⠋ ⛬ ◷ ❖ ⌁ ⟦ ◸              Nerd Font or a rich terminal —
                                          never on a must-read path
 Box-drawing and braille are the safest of all: terminals draw them
 programmatically, so they align whatever the font.

## what to reach for, by the question being answered
 did it work?           ✓ ⚠ ✗
 is it on?              ● ○ ◐
 is it working now?     ⟳ ⠹ ⋯
 which way?             → ↑ ▲ ▼
 how important?         ◆ ◇ ·
 where does it go?      ↳ ⤷ »
 is this a warning?     ⚠            and nothing else, ever

## what a glyph must not do
 ✗  an emoji in a must-read line       width is unpredictable, colour is not yours
 ✗  three glyphs on one line           one per line, two at most in a whole row
 ✗  a glyph instead of a word          "✓" alone is not "build passed"
 ✗  a tier-2 glyph in an error message  the one line that must always render

# paint: verdict glyphs -> their own role colour (ok · warn · danger)
#        marks, sigils and frames -> text.gold or border.hairline
#        field and flow glyphs -> text.muted — they are punctuation, not signal
