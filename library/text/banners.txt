# Banners — section openers and headers. One heavy rule per screen; everything
# else is light. A page with two heavy banners has no hierarchy, only weight.

## sizes — pick by how much of the page the section owns
 a page       the capsule, or the heavy rule
 a section    the numbered rule, or ⧉ S E C T I O N
 a block      [ Section ] or the titled rule
 a paragraph  a light rule, or nothing at all

## what a banner must not do
 ✗  a heavy rule above and below the same title     it is a heading, not a cage
 ✗  a banner wider than the content under it
 ✗  ASCII art as a heading in a must-read path      it does not survive a diff
 ✗  two ceremonial openers on one screen
