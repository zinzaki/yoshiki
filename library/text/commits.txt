# Commits, changelogs and releases — the parts of a project that are read long
# after they are written. Plain text, no colour available, every time.

## a commit subject — the change, not the activity
 ✓  fix(palette): raise kin-2 so dim gold clears 4.5:1 as text
 ✓  feat(kit): a settings row, and the canon entry behind it
 ✗  updates                        what updated, and why
 ✗  fix bug                        which bug
 ✗  WIP                            then do not commit it

## the body — why, not what. The diff already says what.
 fix(palette): raise kin-2 so dim gold clears 4.5:1 as text

 kin-2 was measured only against bg.surface, where it passed at 4.6:1.
 Against bg.app it was 4.33:1 — and bg.app is where labels actually land.

 The proof now measures every text role against every surface, which is
 why this was caught at all.

## conventions worth holding
 · imperative mood: "raise", not "raised" or "raises"
 · one change per commit; a commit that needs "and" is two commits
 · the subject fits in 72 columns, because something will truncate it
 · a breaking change says so in the subject, not only in the body
