## the block quote — someone else's words, kept at arm's length
 │ Nobody would wear a solid-gold watch, but a gilded edge is precious.
 │ So gold lives only as a line, an edge, a frame or a glyph.
 ↑ a left rule, not quotation marks: marks fight the mono grid and wrap badly

## an epigraph — above the thing it frames, smaller than it
     a design language
     ────────────────
     Warm monochrome, struck rarely by colour.

## a terminal quote — a line of output worth stopping on

## what a quote must not do
 ✗  a quote in a coloured box            the calm IS the emphasis
 ✗  italic AND larger AND centred AND gold — pick one
 ✗  a quote that repeats the paragraph above it
 ✗  “smart quotes” around a mono line    they break the column
