# Heatmap — density over two axes. One ramp, five steps at most: the eye
# cannot rank more than that without a legend it has to keep re-reading.

## the ramp — quiet to loud, never a hue rotation
 ▱ ▰ ▰ ▰ ▰      squares, with the empty square as "none"
 ⠀ ░ ▒ ▓ ⣿      density, when the grid is tight
 · ▪ ◆ ◆ ◆      marks, when the cell is one character wide
 ↑ pick ONE and hold it for the whole grid. A ramp that changes hue per step
   is a rainbow, and a rainbow cannot be ordered.
