# Gauge — one value against its whole. Same squares or dot-matrix as bars,
# read as a single fraction.

## the row form — a label, a fraction, a number
 disk   ▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▱▱▱  71%
 load   ▰▰▰▰▰▱▱▱▱▱  58%
 temp   ▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▱  84%  ⚠      past 80%: filled squares -> kaki
 mem    ⣿⣿⣿⣿⣿⣷⠀⠀⠀⠀  63%       dot-matrix variant

## with a threshold mark — the limit is a tick, never a filled band
 temp   ▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▰│▱  84%   limit 90
 quota  ▰▰▰▰▱▱▱│▱▱  42%   soft 70
 ↑ │ sits where the threshold falls; a coloured band would spend colour on
   a region instead of on the one value that crossed it

## the budget form — what is left, not what is spent
 error budget   ▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▱▱▱   68% spent · 9d left
 rate limit     ▰▰▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱   1,204 / 5,000 this hour

## compact — a gauge inside a line of prose or a status bar
 disk 71% ▰▰▰▰▰▰▰▱▱▱ · mem 63% ▰▰▰▰▰▰▱▱▱▱ · load 0.58

## a ring, when a row will not fit
   ╭───╮       ╭───╮       ╭───╮
   │ 24│       │ 71│       │ 96│
   ╰───╯       ╰───╯       ╰───╯
    ▰▱▱▱        ▰▰▰▱        ▰▰▰▰ ⚠
 ↑ the arc is faked with a four-step ramp under the box. Real arcs need
   glyphs that half the terminals in the world do not have.
