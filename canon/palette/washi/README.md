The sepia poster as a whole interface: paper surfaces
(`#E2D6B4 · #EBE1C3 · #F3EBD5`), sumi-ink text (`#2B2117` family),
gold sunk deeper to stay readable — and still only gilding (frames, edges,
text), never a fill. Two inversions keep the language whole:

- the paper artifact card becomes a **lacquer island** (darkness inside light)
- the terminal is **not** inverted — always a kogane-dark island `#0B0A08`

Contrast guards (generated, and enforced against **every** surface a role can
sit on — on paper `bg.hover` is the darkest ground, so that is the real test):
gold as text `#695228`, gold as an edge `#8A6B39`, scarlet text `#9A3027`, moss
text `#415D31`. The hover gold `#755C2B` is *darker* than the resting gold, not
lighter: on paper a stronger seam sinks, and hover must strengthen it in both
worlds. See [`../CONTRAST.md`](../CONTRAST.md).
