# Workshop

References come in, get taken apart, and leave as material for the foundation.

- `inbox/` — photos, screenshots and links as they arrive. **Never committed**: the images belong
  to other people.
- `refs/<id>/analysis.md` — one analysis per reference, id `yk:ref/NNNN`: what it is made of
  (material, colour, type, grid, interface devices, effects, motion), why it may appeal, how it
  relates to other references and earlier votes, and the draft components it yields.

An analysis reasons about causes, not frequency: a motif that appears often in the inbox is not
therefore preferred. Conclusions are hypotheses until a voting round confirms them. Draft components
link back with `derived-from: [yk:ref/NNNN]` and enter canon only after a vote.
